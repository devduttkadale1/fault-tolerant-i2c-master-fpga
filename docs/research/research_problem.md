# Research Problem

## Current Problem

I2C master controllers are widely implemented in FPGA and RTL systems.
However, reliable operation under abnormal bus conditions requires
additional monitoring, fault detection and recovery mechanisms.

## Current Status

The final research problem is NOT yet frozen.

It will be selected after completion of the literature analysis.

## Candidate Areas

- stuck SDA detection
- stuck SCL detection
- clock-stretch monitoring
- timeout mechanisms
- fault classification
- recovery strategy
- false-positive avoidance
- recovery latency
- hardware overhead
- reliability/PPA trade-offs

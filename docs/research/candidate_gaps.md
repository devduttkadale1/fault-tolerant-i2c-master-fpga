# Candidate Research Gaps

> These are hypotheses only. They are not novelty claims.

## Candidate 1 — Fault Classification

Can different I2C bus abnormalities be classified before selecting
a recovery mechanism?

## Candidate 2 — Clock Stretching vs Fault

Can legitimate clock stretching be distinguished from persistent
fault conditions without introducing excessive hardware overhead?

## Candidate 3 — Recovery Latency vs Hardware Cost

What hardware overhead is required to achieve bounded fault detection
and recovery latency?

## Candidate 4 — False Positive Analysis

How frequently does a timeout-based mechanism incorrectly classify
legitimate I2C behavior as a fault?

## Candidate 5 — Configurable Recovery

Can recovery policies be made configurable while maintaining
acceptable FPGA resource and timing overhead?

These candidates require further literature analysis before selecting
the final research contribution.
